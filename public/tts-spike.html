Manual speech test

Paste a completed response below. Nothing plays until you press Speak. This experiment is separate from chat and does not read your conversation.

Voices depend on your browser and operating system. Remote voices may send text to a speech service. Select a voice marked local when testing local synthesis.

Idle — audio off
